\section*{Summary}
TIDE is an ARC cache in which a $12\to64\to1$ MLP, trained online from delayed reuse-distance labels, picks the victim
among ARC's chosen list's 4 LRU keys, its MRU key and (on a new-key miss) the incoming key. A shadow ARC hands control
back when TIDE falls behind. This run compares it with LRU, LFU, ARC, S3-FIFO, SIEVE, LIRS and three learned policies
(LeCaR, Cacheus, LRB; libCacheSim reference implementations) on synthetic and real traces.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Synthetic traces} (\NGroupsSynth{} trace/size groups, \Instances{} generator instances, \Seeds{} TIDE seeds):
        TIDE ranks \#\RankSynthTIDE{} with mean normalised hit rate \NormSynthTIDE{}, against \NormSynthARC{} (ARC),
        \NormSynthCacheus{} (Cacheus), \NormSynthSthreeFIFO{} (S3-FIFO) and \NormSynthLIRS{} (LIRS).
        Its mean hit rate is \DSynthARC{} points above ARC \DSynthARCCI, \DSynthSthree{} above S3-FIFO \DSynthSthreeCI{}
        and \DSynthLIRS{} above LIRS \DSynthLIRSCI.
  \item \textbf{Real traces} (MSR, Twitter, Meta CDN; first \RealN{} requests each): TIDE ranks \#\RankRealTIDE.
        Its mean hit rate is \DRealARC{} points against ARC \DRealARCCI{} and \DRealSthree{} against S3-FIFO \DRealSthreeCI.
        TIDE is at least as good as ARC in \GeARCReal{} of \NGroupsReal{} settings; worst \WorstReal{} (\WorstCellReal).
        The model was active for only \ActiveRealMin--\ActiveRealMax\% of requests because the shadow-ARC fallback kept switching it off.
  \item \textbf{Speed} (per request, 1\% cache): p99 falls from \PNineNineRef\,\textmu s (NumPy) to \PNineNineSync\,\textmu s (Numba, inline training)
        and \PNineNineAsync\,\textmu s (Numba, asynchronous training); median \PFiftyAsync\,\textmu s against ARC's \PFiftyARC\,\textmu s.
        Throughput is \SpeedupAsync$\times$ the NumPy version and still \SlowdownAsync$\times$ below ARC.
  \item \textbf{Asynchronous training} costs at most \AsyncOthers{} points on four of five traces, but \AsyncWorst{} on \AsyncWorstTrace.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Caveats}
\begin{itemize}
  \item This is the reduced ($\approx$\,10-minute) configuration: 2 generator instances, 3 TIDE seeds, 500k-request trend traces
        and 1M-request prefixes of the real traces. LRB in particular needs long histories and may be under-trained on short traces.
  \item Confidence intervals measure run-to-run noise (instances and seeds) for this set of workloads, not how the results would generalise to other workloads.
  \item Real traces are evaluated with unit object sizes (object hit rate); byte hit rate is not measured.
  \item LFU is the standard in-cache LFU; \texttt{LFU*} (global counts, unbounded metadata) is recorded in the CSVs but left out.
\end{itemize}
